About Me

My Journey

I'm an early-career data analyst passionate about transforming raw data into actionable insights. My journey into data analytics began with a curiosity about how businesses make informed decisions, and has evolved into a commitment to mastering the tools and techniques that drive data-driven strategies.

What I Do

I specialize in database management with PostgreSQL, creating compelling visualizations in Power BI, performing complex analyses in Excel, and building automated workflows with N8N. I believe that good data analysis isn't just about the numbers—it's about telling the story behind them and empowering stakeholders to make better decisions.

Beyond the Data

When I'm not analyzing datasets or building dashboards, I'm constantly learning new tools and techniques in the ever-evolving field of data analytics. I'm actively seeking opportunities to apply my skills in challenging projects and contribute to data-driven decision making in dynamic organizations.
